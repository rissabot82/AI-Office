# AI Office

**Current Version:** v2.4.0 — Discord Mobile Operations  
**Status:** Certified / Operational  
**Platform:** Windows + WSL2 + OpenClaw + Ollama  
**Remote Interface:** Live Discord  
**Next Release:** v2.5.0 — Intelligence Upgrade

AI Office is a private, modular, local-first AI operations platform designed to coordinate executive planning, specialized departments, persistent knowledge, autonomous workflows, personal operations, business development, connected services, and cross-department reasoning from a locally controlled environment.

The project has progressed from an AI assistant framework into a functioning **self-hosted hybrid AI operations platform** with local inference, autonomous orchestration, persistent services, conversational intake, and a live remote Discord interface.

---

## Current Operational Architecture

AI Office currently includes:

### Chief of Staff
Central coordination layer responsible for intake, prioritization, planning, routing, and cross-department coordination.

### Long-Term Memory
Persistent structured memory for reusable operational knowledge, historical context, prior decisions, and approved preferences.

### Knowledge Graph & Reasoning
Structured entities, relationships, provenance, inference, contradiction detection, context ranking, and decision scoring.

### Multi-Agent Collaboration
Infrastructure for specialized agents and departments to collaborate, hand off work, review results, and resolve conflicts.

### Autonomous Workflows
Goal execution, workflow state management, approvals, checkpoints, retries, recovery, background workers, and monitoring.

### Personal Financial Office
Structured financial management for accounts, bills, income, debts, savings goals, paycheck planning, cash-flow forecasting, side-hustle performance, and recommendations.

### Business Incubator
Framework for idea capture, opportunity scoring, research, validation experiments, launch planning, and business-development workflows.

### Self-Hosted AI Runtime
Operational local inference through Ollama, OpenClaw gateway integration, hybrid routing, workload optimization, resilience, failover, recovery, and health monitoring.

### Conversational AI Office
Persistent conversation sessions, message history, conversational turns, Chief-of-Staff-first intake, local inference, and interactive PowerShell conversation.

### Discord Mobile Operations
Private remote access through Discord with explicit allowlists, persistent sessions, commands, department routing, worker services, operations monitoring, safety/audit controls, and live activation readiness.

---

## Repository Safety

Do not commit:

- API keys
- Bot tokens
- Passwords
- Session cookies
- Customer or lead data
- Sensitive financial information
- Runtime conversation history
- Generated operational logs
- Local installer backups

The repository is the engineering source of truth. Private operational data remains local unless explicitly approved for another secure destination.
