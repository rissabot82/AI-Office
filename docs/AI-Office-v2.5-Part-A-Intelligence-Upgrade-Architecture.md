# AI Office v2.5 Part A — Intelligence Upgrade Architecture

## Objective

Part A establishes the quality architecture for AI Office intelligence.

The purpose is not to replace working Discord or conversational infrastructure. It creates a benchmark-driven layer for deciding which models should handle which kinds of work.

## Core Principle

AI Office should use the least resource-intensive model that can still meet the required quality tier.

## Quality Tiers

### Fast
Simple conversation, classification, extraction, formatting, and utility work.

### Standard
General drafting, planning, summarization, marketing ideation, creative work, and ordinary analysis.

### High
Complex reasoning, coding, strategic work, specialized department work, and tasks where poor quality has a meaningful cost.

## Part A Deliverables

- Intelligence policy
- Quality-tier definitions
- Task-family definitions
- Baseline benchmark suite
- Model capability schema
- Installed-model inventory
- Architecture validation

## What Part A Does Not Do

Part A does not change the live conversational model yet.

That is intentional. The current v2.4 runtime remains the rollback-safe production baseline while v2.5 benchmarks and improves intelligence separately.

## Planned v2.5 Sequence

- Part A — Intelligence Upgrade Architecture
- Part B — Local Model Inventory & Benchmark Harness
- Part C — Model Quality Benchmarking
- Part D — Intelligent Model Selection
- Part E — Conversational Runtime Integration
- Part F — Department Intelligence Profiles
- Part G — Quality Escalation & Fallback
- Part H — Certification & Release
