# AI Office Vision

## Purpose

AI Office exists to reduce the mental load of managing work, finances, side income, creative projects, future businesses, and personal administration.

It should function as a dependable local executive organization rather than a collection of disconnected chatbots.

## North Star

A completed AI Office can receive a goal from a phone or computer, understand the relevant people and projects, recall prior decisions, create a plan, delegate work, execute approved actions, monitor progress, recover from interruptions, request human approval when needed, and report the result through one control center.

## Core Principles

- Local-first operation
- Human-supervised autonomy
- Memory with provenance
- Modular departments
- Recoverable execution
- Observable operations
- Security by design
- Practical value first

## Long-Term Outcome

AI Office becomes an autonomous AI enterprise that coordinates professional work, personal administration, financial planning, side hustles, and new ventures under one executive operating model.

After v2.0, the system should become self-hosting: able to design, generate, validate, test, package, install, document, monitor, and roll back its own upgrades under human supervision.
